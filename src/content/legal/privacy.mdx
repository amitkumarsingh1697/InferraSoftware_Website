This policy explains what personal information Inferra Software ("we", "us") collects through this website, why, and what choices you have. It describes the data flows the website actually implements.

## Who we are

Inferra Software is a software engineering company founded by Amit Kumar Singh. You can contact us about this policy by phone on the number shown on our [contact page](/contact). Our registered legal entity details and a privacy contact email will be added here once confirmed.

## Information we collect

### When you send an enquiry

If you use the enquiry form, we collect the information you choose to provide:

- your name, business email address, company name and, optionally, your job title;
- your country or region;
- the service, project type and engagement model you are interested in;
- the description of your challenge, and, optionally, a budget range, timeline and a link to your CV or portfolio;
- how you heard about us;
- your confirmation that you have read this policy.

Please do not include passwords, credentials or confidential information in the form.

### Campaign and referral information

When you first arrive on the site, your browser keeps a small record in **session storage** (not a cookie) of any campaign parameters in the link you followed (such as `utm_source`), the domain of the website that referred you, and the first page you visited. This record stays on your device and is only sent to us if you submit an enquiry. It is deleted automatically when you close your browser.

### Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider processes technical information such as IP addresses, browser type and request times in server logs, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Our enquiry system uses a one-way hash of your IP address, held briefly in memory, to limit repeated submissions. We do not store raw IP addresses alongside enquiries.

### Analytics

Analytics are **disabled** unless we enable them. If enabled, we use a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics service that measures aggregate usage, such as page views and clicks on call-to-action buttons, without identifying individual visitors. This section will be updated with the provider's name if analytics are switched on.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not sell personal information.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- respond to your enquiry and discuss potential work, partnerships or roles;
- understand which pages and campaigns help people find us, in aggregate;
- protect the website and enquiry form from abuse and spam.

Where data protection laws require a legal basis, we rely on your consent (for enquiries you choose to send) and our legitimate interests in running and securing our business. This will be confirmed during legal review for the jurisdictions where we operate.

## Who we share it with

Enquiries are delivered to us using service providers that process data on our behalf, such as our website hosting provider and a transactional email provider. Where a provider is located outside your country, appropriate safeguards will be used as required by applicable law. The specific providers will be listed here once configured.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights and the security of our services.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting relationship, and then delete it. Career enquiries are kept for consideration for future opportunities unless you ask us to delete them. Exact retention periods will be confirmed during legal review.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details on our contact page. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, restricted access to enquiry data and keeping secrets out of our source code. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information through the form.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
